The 'Arrogante Passion' Trilogy

This contemporary romance trilogy required a cohesive visual identity that evolves alongside the characters' journey.

The design focuses on the harmony between bold typography, a consistent color palette, and the strategic use of icons that hint at key plot points.

The goal was to ensure instant series recognition while establishing a strong visual brand for the titles.
