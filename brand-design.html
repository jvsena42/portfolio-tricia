Uaibit Logo

Project: Uaibit

Concept: Tradition Meets Technology

The visual identity for Uaibit bridges the gap between the regional heritage of Minas Gerais and the global digital asset ecosystem.

The design leverages the iconic mountain silhouettes as a foundation for growth, integrating the Bitcoin symbol as a rising sun to signal a new technological horizon.

Bold yet approachable typography, combined with a strategic color split in the naming, reinforces the brand's accessibility - merging local cultural warmth with the cutting-edge nature of decentralized finance.

Project: LauroOnTheRoad

Concept: Smart Living on the Move

The project was designed for a 'van life' traveler who converted a Smart Car into a multifunctional home on wheels.

The challenge was to create a cohesive visual identity that segmented the vehicle's various uses: Smart Studio for content creation, Smart Kitchen for nomadic cooking, and Smart Gym for fitness.

Through classic typography and minimalist iconography, the design highlights spatial efficiency and organization, proving that meaningful experiences don't require large square footage.

Bitcoin Beauty School

Project: Bitcoin Beauty School

Concept: Education Through Analogy

Created during a Designathon, Bitcoin Beauty School is an educational ecosystem tailored for women.

The project leverages design as a bridge to demystify the crypto world, replacing technical complexity with relatable everyday analogies - such as comparing makeup 'foundation' to fundamental Bitcoin concepts.

Through a light interface, vibrant tones, and gamified quizzes, the goal was to turn BTC learning into an intuitive experience, breaking down gender barriers and bringing women closer to decentralized finance in an organic, approachable way.

Project: Satoshiback

Concept: Bridging the Gap to Satoshi

Created for the Bitcoin++ hackathon, Satoshiback is an AI agent designed to simulate a direct conversation with the true Satoshi Nakamoto.

The project's goal was to build an interface where users could ask questions and discuss protocol fundamentals as if they were speaking with the creator himself.

The design employs a professional, corporate aesthetic, with the Bitcoin symbol replacing the face to represent the idea that, within a decentralized ecosystem, individual identity is superseded by the power of technology and code.

Tricia Linewberg Designer Logo

Project: Linewberg Design

Concept: Design Company

My personal identity was crafted to reflect the fusion of classic design and digital interactivity. The logo features ornamental flourishes reminiscent of the authority and handcrafted feel of a traditional stamp, contrasted by a computer cursor that symbolizes action and the digital environment where my projects come to life.

With bold typography and a sober color palette, the brand establishes a balance between aesthetic sophistication and technical precision, positioning my work as a bridge between visual tradition and the new frontiers of technology.
